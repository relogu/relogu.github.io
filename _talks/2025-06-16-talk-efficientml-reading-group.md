On the 16th of June 2025, Francesco Corti invited me to present Photon, our system for federated pre-training of large language models, at the [EfficientML reading group](https://sites.google.com/view/efficientml) of TU Graz, a public online ELLIS reading group on the mathematics and efficiency of deep learning.
The recording is available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/219kviGMU40).
For more details regarding this project please visit the [project page](https://relogu.github.io/publication/2024-11-05-photon-system-paper).
