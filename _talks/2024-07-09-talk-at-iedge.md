On the 7th July 2024, I helped [Javier Fernandez-Marques](https://jafermarq.com/) presenting a keytone with the title "Federating Everything with Flower" at [iEDGE Symposium - FOUNDATION MODELS AT THE EDGE](https://edge.conferences.computer.org/2024/iedge-symposium/).
In particular, I presented a few insights of our earlier work on large language models and how [Flower](https://flower.ai/) can be used to federate them.
For more details regarding this project please visit the [project page](https://relogu.github.io/publication/2024-05-17-photon-tech-report).
