On the 2nd of August 2024, I took part in the first FlowerLLM AMA, an online Ask-Me-Anything session run by [Flower Labs](https://flower.ai/) after the release of FlowerLLM-3B, answering questions from the Flower community about our work on federated pre-training of large language models.
For more details regarding this project please visit the [project page](https://relogu.github.io/publication/2024-05-17-photon-tech-report).
