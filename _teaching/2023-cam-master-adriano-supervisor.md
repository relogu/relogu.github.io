I supervised **Adriano Guastella** (from the University of Bologna) during his Master's Thesis project when he was visiting the Computer Laboratory. The work investigated the intersection between Powerpropagation, Sparse Weight Activation Training, and federated learning.

The main research project will be described in an arXiv paper available soon.

The preliminary results were presented at [MobiUK 2024](https://mobiuk.org/2024/).
The abstract of the presentation can be found [here](https://mobiuk.org/2024/abstract/S4_P1_Guastella_SparseFedPP.pdf).
