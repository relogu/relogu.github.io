A Cambridge Department of Computer Science and Technology news piece covering the Red Queen Gödel Machine, a framework that lets AI agents and their evaluators co-evolve rather than compete against a fixed benchmark. The approach overcomes performance plateaus by continuously raising evaluation standards, showing gains in scientific paper writing and grading while potentially cutting computational cost through a hybrid of open-source and proprietary models.
