Flower Labs launched Endeavor 1.0, a frontier-class AI model designed to stay deployable within organizations' own infrastructure rather than locking them into a managed API. It achieves competitive performance across key benchmarks and can be accessed through Flower's managed service or through private deployment, giving organizations a path from API dependency to independent operation as their needs evolve.
